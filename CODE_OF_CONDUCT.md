# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in IssueLight a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and correct harm.
- Protect private information and security reports.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
options on the GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports
will be handled with appropriate confidentiality.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
